Software Engineering Assessment  
The Lost & Found Matcher  
The Scenario  
Imagine a university with a very simple lost-and-found system.  
Students can submit two kinds of reports:  
**Lost Item**  
“I lost my black AirPods case yesterday near the cafeteria.  
”  
**Found Item**  
“Found a dark wireless earbud case beside the coffee shop.  
”  
The university receives many reports like these, but currently someone has to manually read through  
them and figure out which ones might match.  
Your Task  
Build a small application that helps identify **potential matches between lost and found reports**.  
That’s it.  
How you solve the problem is up to you.  
You may decide:  
what information a report should contain;  
how lost and found items are compared;  
what makes two reports a possible match;  
whether some matches should be considered stronger than others;  
how results should be presented to the user;  
how incorrect, incomplete, or unusual input should be handled.  
There is intentionally no precise definition of a “match.  
” Part of the exercise is deciding what you think  
a reasonable solution should do.  
Example  
A user might report:  
**Lost:** Black backpack containing a laptop charger. Lost around the library on Monday afternoon.  
And another person might report:  
**Found:** Dark-colored backpack found near the library entrance Monday evening.Your application might consider these a potential match.  
On the other hand:  
**Found:** Black backpack found at the football field two weeks later.  
may or may not be a good match.  
Your application should make that determination in whatever way you believe is appropriate.  
What You Can Use

AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Codex, etc.  
Google  
Stack Overflow  
open-source libraries  
APIs  
frameworks  
documentation  
any programming language you prefer  
**Using AI will not reduce your score.**  
We are interested in how you use the tools available to you to solve a problem.  
What We Expect  
Please keep the application small.  
We are **not** looking for a production-ready system or a large feature set.  
A simple application that makes thoughtful decisions is preferable to a complicated application with  
many unfinished features.  
At minimum, we should be able to:  
1\. Create or provide lost-item reports.  
2\. Create or provide found-item reports.  
3\. See potential matches.  
Everything beyond that is your decision.  
Time  
This should take **around 3 hours**. That is a guide rather than a hard limit, so there is no need to stop  
mid-thought or rush to finish.We intentionally want the scope to be small.  
If there are things you would improve or build with more time, explain them rather than trying to  
finish everything.  
Submission  
Please submit:  
1\. Your solution  
Provide the source code, screenshots of the running app and instructions for running it.  
2\. A short README  
Please briefly explain:  
the approach you took;  
important assumptions you made;

major technical decisions you made;  
anything you intentionally chose **not** to build;

”

A Few Notes  
There are intentionally unanswered questions in this problem.  
You do not need to contact us every time you encounter one.  
Make reasonable assumptions, document them, and continue.  
We care much more about **how you think and make decisions** than about how many features you  
build.  
Have fun with it.
